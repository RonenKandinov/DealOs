Next gap to attack: the observation error, before a value objective.

Persistent bias and fresh noise are not interchangeable. FIFO and Bottleneck size each allocation to the observed remaining gap. A constant positive offset makes that gap short by exactly the offset, so true progress stalls at 1 − b, the task then looks complete, and the deal never finishes. Wasted allocation stays 0 and idle-despite rises above 0.5. A constant negative offset does the opposite: finished tasks still look open, wasted allocation rises above 300, and completions fall to about 0. Fresh noise redraws the error every morning, so the shortfall is not permanent: FIFO still finishes 3.58 deals at magnitude 0.05 and 1.86 at 0.10, with wasted allocation between those two bias arms. The comparison exceeds the screening band at +0.05, −0.05, +0.10, and −0.10. A deal-level bias shared across tasks, rather than the same offset on every task, was not tested.
